# Geoheritage of a fictional coastal plateau

## Abstract

We inventory twelve geosites on a fictional plateau and rank them by scientific value.

## Introduction

Geoheritage inventories support geoconservation planning.

## Methods

Sites were selected during two field campaigns. <!-- REVISION R1-1 -->Selection followed the criteria of representativeness, integrity and rarity defined by Brilha (2016) [@brilha2016].<!-- END REVISION -->

## Results

Twelve geosites were identified. Site A scored highest.

<!-- REVISION R2-1 -->
### Limitations of the ranking

- Scores reflect a single assessor.
- Accessibility was not weighted.
<!-- END REVISION -->

## Discussion

The plateau has potential for geotourism.
